dBm to milliwatts

dBm is defined against the milliwatt, so converting dBm back to milliwatts is the most direct conversion in RF: 0 dBm is exactly 1 mW, 10 dBm is 10 mW, and 20 dBm is 100 mW. A Bluetooth radio at 10 dBm transmits 10 mW; a Wi-Fi card at 20 dBm pushes 100 mW. Signal generators and receiver sensitivity specs live entirely in this range.

dBm to milliwatts

The formula P(mW) = 10^(dBm/10) is exact — dBm means decibels relative to one milliwatt, so the definition itself is the conversion. The practical range is enormous: receiver sensitivity sits around −90 dBm (a picowatt), while transmitters run +20 to +40 dBm. This calculator handles the transmitter side, where mW is a readable number, and it is the unit most test equipment agrees on.

milliwatts = 10^(dBm / 10)

Frequently Asked Questions

How many milliwatts is 0 dBm?

Exactly 1 milliwatt — that is the definition of dBm. Each 10 dB multiplies the milliwatts by 10: 10 dBm = 10 mW, 20 dBm = 100 mW.

What power does Bluetooth transmit?

Class 1 Bluetooth transmits at 20 dBm (100 mW); Class 2 — the common low-power mode — at 4 dBm (2.5 mW). This converter covers both with the same formula.

How do I convert dBm to mW by hand?

For every 10 dB add a zero: 0 dBm = 1 mW, 10 dBm = 10 mW, 20 dBm = 100 mW. For in-between values use 10^(dBm/10) exactly.
