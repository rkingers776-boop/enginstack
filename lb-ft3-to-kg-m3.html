Frequently Asked Questions

How do I estimate kg/m³ from lb/ft³ quickly?

Multiply by 16. For a quick estimate, 150 lb/ft³ × 16 = 2,400 kg/m³ — the concrete number everyone remembers. The exact factor is 16.018463, so the shortcut is accurate to about 0.1%.

Why does US construction use lb/ft³?

Because the US building codes and test standards were written in customary units, and the density values became memorable: 62.4 for water, 150 for concrete, 110 for soil. The industry stays bilingual and converts.

Is 62.4 lb/ft³ water or 62.428?

62.4 is the shorthand; the exact value for water at 4 °C is 62.427961 lb/ft³. The 0.04% difference is irrelevant for concrete mix design but matters in hydrology and naval architecture.
