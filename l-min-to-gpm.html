liters per minute to US gallons per minute

European plumbing fixtures are rated in liters per minute; American ones in gallons per minute — same faucet, two unit worlds. The bridge is 0.2641720523581484, the exact number of US gallons in one liter, carried forward per minute. A kitchen faucet that flows 8 L/min is a 2.11 GPM faucet; a European shower spec of 12 L/min is 3.17 GPM on the American box.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many GPM is 1 L/min?

Exactly 0.2641720524. One US gallon is 3.785411784 L by definition (231 in³), so one liter per minute is 1/3.785411784 gallons per minute.

Is the imperial gallon different for flow rates?

Yes — the imperial gallon is 4.54609 L, about 20% larger than the US gallon. A flow rated in imperial GPM is 1.2× the same number in US GPM. Always confirm which gallon a pump spec uses.

How do I convert L/min to GPM by hand?

Multiply by 0.264 for a mental estimate (within 0.07%). For exact work use 0.2641720523581484, or divide by 3.785411784 — the two operations are identical.
