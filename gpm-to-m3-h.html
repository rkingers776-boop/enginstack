US gallons per minute to cubic meters per hour

The factor chains two exact definitions: the US gallon is 3.785411784 liters, and 1 L/min = 0.06 m³/h, so 1 GPM = 3.785411784 × 0.06 = 0.22712470704 m³/h. Fire protection, chiller water loops, and industrial water supply all cross this boundary when an American spec meets an international one — and because both anchors are definitions, the factor is exact to eleven digits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many m³/h is 1 GPM?

Exactly 0.22712470704. One US gallon is 3.785411784 L, and 1 L/min = 0.06 m³/h, so 1 GPM = 3.785411784 × 0.06 m³/h.

Why does fire protection use GPM in the US?

Because US fire codes and pump ratings were built on the gallon; international standards quote m³/h or L/min. A 500 GPM fire pump and a 113.6 m³/h pump are the same machine with two labels.

How do I convert GPM to m³/h by hand?

Multiply by 0.227 for a mental estimate, or divide by 4.4028675393 — the exact reciprocal — for spec-level work.
