Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the factor exactly 62.427961?

Because 1 g/cm³ is the density of water, and water's density in US customary units is 62.427961 lb/ft³. The number carries the 1959 pound and foot, cubed.

Do I multiply or divide for steel?

Steel is 7.85 g/cm³ in metric tables and about 490 lb/ft³ in US practice. Metric-to-US, multiply by 62.427961: 7.85 × 62.4 = 490. The 62.4 shortcut is accurate to 0.05%.

Why do US fabricators want lb/ft³?

Because plate weight is estimated as volume × density, and US shop practice works in inches and pounds. A 1-inch steel plate weighs 40.8 lb/ft² — derived from 490 ÷ 12.
