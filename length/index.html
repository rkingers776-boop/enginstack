Inches

Bolt sizes, sheet metal gauges, bearing bores — metric parts in imperial shops. ÷ 25.4 Inches to Feet 12 in = 1 ft. Construction framing, lumber lengths, human height. Feet

International building code translation. Metric blueprints for US contractors. ÷ 0.3048 Feet to Centimeters 1 ft = 30.48 cm. Human height for medical records. Fitness, health, apparel. ÷ 30.48 Feet to Millimeters 1 ft = 304.8 mm. Precision manufacturing from architectural drawings. Concrete pours, landscaping, sports field dimensions. Every US contractor knows this cold. ÷ 3 Yards to Feet 1 yd = 3 ft. Excavation volumes, turf orders, pipeline trenching. Astronomical
