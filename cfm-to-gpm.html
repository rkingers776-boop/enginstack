cubic feet per minute to US gallons per minute

A cubic foot holds exactly 7.4805194805 US gallons — the number every tank and pool calculator uses — so a cubic foot per minute of anything is 7.4805194805 gallons per minute. Compressed air ratings in CFM, cooling tower air flows in CFM, and water flows in GPM all meet at this factor in HVAC and industrial plants, where the same fan moves air that engineers must compare to the water side.

cubic feet per minute to US gallons per minute

The factor is exact: one cubic foot is 1,728 in³ and one US gallon is 231 in³, so one cubic foot holds 1,728/231 = 7.4805194805 gallons. Per minute, that is the CFM-to-GPM factor. It is the reciprocal of the 0.13368 that goes the other way, and it is the only non-decimal flow constant on this site because it lives entirely inside the inch world — no liter, no meter, just the two inch-volume definitions and a minute.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many GPM is 1 CFM?

Exactly 7.4805194805. A cubic foot is 1,728 in³ and a US gallon is 231 in³, so the ratio is exact.

Why is 7.48 such a famous number?

Because it converts cubic feet to US gallons — the anchor of every aquarium, pool, and tank volume calculation in America. 'A cubic foot of water is 7.48 gallons and weighs 62.4 pounds' is a mantra of the trade.

Can I use 7.5 as a shortcut?

Yes, for mental math — 7.5 is within 0.26%. For tank sizing and process balances, use the full 7.4805194805, since the error compounds across large volumes and flows.
