hectares to square feet

The hectare is the international unit of farmland and large development sites — 10,000 square meters, the area of a square 100 meters on a side. American building and real-estate still speak square feet, and the bridge between them is 107,639.104167: the exact number of square feet in one hectare, derived from the 1959 foot. A two-hectare site is 215,278 ft²; a hectare-sized solar farm is about 107,639 ft² of panels.

hectares to square feet

One hectare is exactly 107,639.104167 square feet. The factor is a pure product of definitions: a hectare is 10,000 m² by definition, and a square meter is 10.76391041671 ft² because the foot is 0.3048 m — square it and multiply by 10,000. The result has eleven digits because the conversion from a decimal metric area to an English-area unit cannot come out clean. Development sites, campus plans, and agricultural leases all cross this boundary when a metric country's documents meet a US contractor's takeoffs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet are in a hectare?

Exactly 107,639.104167. A hectare is 10,000 m²; a square meter is (1/0.3048)² = 10.76391041671 ft² because the foot is exactly 0.3048 m; multiply to get 107,639.104167 ft².

Why is the hectare-to-square-feet number so large?

Because a hectare is a genuinely large area — 100 m × 100 m — while the square foot is a small building unit. The 107,639 factor is just the ratio of a big metric area unit to a small customary one; there is no rounding or error in it.

What is a hectare in acres?

One hectare = 2.4710538147 acres. Since both are land units of similar scale, this is the conversion farmers actually use day to day — the hectare-to-square-feet number mostly appears in development and construction takeoffs.
