Frequently Asked Questions

Is 490 lb/ft³ steel or 489?

Structural steel is commonly specified at 490 lb/ft³ (about 7.85 g/cm³). The exact value varies slightly with alloy — carbon steel is about 7.83-7.87 g/cm³. For certified work use the alloy's exact value; for estimates, 490 is the industry standard.

How do I convert lb/ft³ to g/cm³ quickly?

Divide by 62.4: 150 ÷ 62.4 = 2.40 g/cm³ (concrete); 490 ÷ 62.4 = 7.85 g/cm³ (steel). The exact factor is 62.427961, making the shortcut accurate to 0.05%.

Why does the same material have two memorable numbers?

Each industry picked the unit that made its numbers round. Metric materials engineering got 2.7 for aluminum and 7.85 for steel in g/cm³. US construction got 169 and 490 in lb/ft³. The 62.4 bridge is exact.
